# IBM HR Attrition & Workforce Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset to explore workforce characteristics and identify patterns associated with employee attrition.

The analysis focuses on workforce composition, department and job-role attrition, overtime, job satisfaction, employee tenure, income, and other workforce factors to generate actionable HR insights and business recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Python – Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis
- SQL – Data Analysis and Business Queries
- Power BI – Data Visualization and Interactive Dashboard
- Excel – Dataset Handling

## Key KPIs
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Who Left: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Average Age: 36.92 years
- Average Years at Company: 7.01 years

## Key Insights
- Employees working overtime showed a substantially higher attrition rate than employees not working overtime.
- Employees with the lowest job satisfaction level showed higher attrition.
- Early-tenure employees, particularly those with 0–2 years at the company, showed higher attrition.
- Attrition patterns varied across job roles and departments.
- Around 60% of employees who left belonged to Job Level 1.
- Research & Development represents the largest share of the workforce.
- Average monthly income increases across higher job levels.

## Business Recommendations
- Review overtime workload and staffing requirements.
- Strengthen onboarding and early-tenure employee support.
- Investigate drivers of low job satisfaction.
- Develop role-specific retention strategies.
- Monitor both attrition counts and attrition rates.
- Use high-risk employee segments for proactive HR review.
